# Strategist Dashboard — Apple

## Investment regime

**Mixed transition.** Apple is gaining premium share and delivering double-digit iPhone/Services growth, while global smartphone units decline and component costs rise. EU distribution rules are an additional structural uncertainty. [OBS-002, OBS-009, OBS-010, OBS-011]

## Earnings direction

**Neutral to positive, with decelerating growth.** The base model has FY2027 revenue growth of 6.5% after FY2026E's 14.6%, because it normalizes tariff-refund benefit and does not assume FQ3 product-cycle momentum persists. FQ4 guidance is 9%-11% growth and 46%-47% adjusted gross margin. [A-001, A-002, DER-004]

## Stance

**No benchmark-relative rating.** The most important monitor is FQ4 adjusted gross margin, with Services growth relative to company growth as the complementary test. The valuation methods are too divergent and unvalidated for an S&P 500-relative call. [CON-004, F-002]
