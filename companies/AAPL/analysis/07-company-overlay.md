# Company Overlay and Forecasts — Apple

## Architecture

Apple aggregates hardware sales into iPhone, Mac, iPad, Wearables/Home/Accessories and Services. FY2025 revenue was $416.2B; iPhone was the largest category at $209.6B, while Services was $109.2B and disproportionately profitable. Geographic disclosures show a global business with material Americas, Europe and Greater China exposure. [OBS-004, OBS-005, S-003]

## Recent performance

FQ3 2026 revenue was $109.4B (+16% YoY), powered by iPhone (+22%), Mac (+29%) and Services (+12%). This is a strong starting point, but reported company gross margin was aided by about 200 bp of tariff refunds. The forecast normalizes that benefit rather than treating it as a recurring margin driver. [OBS-001, OBS-002, OBS-003, DER-001]

## Recommendation logic

* Earnings direction is constructive in the base case, but decelerates sharply from FQ3's reported growth once cycle, comparison and tariff-refund effects normalize.
* Cash generation, Services mix and net cash support a premium valuation.
* The current 36.1x trailing P/E leaves limited room for error on durable growth and margin.
* The corrected probability-weighted P/E scenario value is $313.9, effectively the $314.70 snapshot price; the illustrative DCF is lower at $237.2.

**No benchmark-relative rating.** The P/E and DCF methods differ materially, while peer, consensus and EU-exposure work remains incomplete. Preserve this ambiguity rather than force an Equal-weight call. This is not personalized investment advice. [CON-004, G-001]

## Falsification

The valuation range should become more cautious if FQ4 adjusted gross margin is below 46%, if FQ1 remains below 46.5%, or if Services growth falls below total-company growth. Upside requires the reverse: visible premium share gains, Services growth above company growth and no material loss of EU ecosystem economics. [CAT-001, F-002, R-002]
