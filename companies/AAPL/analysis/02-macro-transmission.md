# Macro Transmission — Apple

## Transmission map

| Macro variable | Operating KPI | Revenue / margin transmission | Current state |
|---|---|---|---|
| Memory, NAND and DRAM inflation | Product BOM and product GM | Higher component cost → lower product GM unless price/mix offsets it → lower EBIT and FCF | Active and expected to intensify [OBS-009] |
| Smartphone unit cycle | Upgrade units and premium share | Fewer industry units → hardware revenue risk; premium share gains can offset | Q2 global units down 7.4%, but Apple gained share [OBS-010] |
| FX | Reported international sales and margin | Local sales translated into USD; hedges reduce, rather than remove, exposures | Company has material FX hedges and cites currency fluctuations as a risk [S-001, S-003] |
| Tariffs / trade policy | Product COGS and pricing | Tariff cost or refund → product GM and EPS; pricing mitigation can affect demand | FQ3 benefited from refunds; future effect is uncertain [OBS-003] |
| Rates | Equity duration, investment portfolio and debt expense | Higher rates raise discount rate and can raise debt expense; DCF value is more sensitive than earnings | 100-bp rate shock in FY2025 disclosure: $129M annual debt-interest impact [S-003] |

## Quantified valuation sensitivity

The illustrative DCF uses $130.5B to $174.9B of FY2026E-FY2030E FCF, 3.0% terminal growth and $62.2B of net cash. Holding those fully persisted cash flows and terminal growth constant, a 100-bp lower/higher WACC changes value from approximately **$237 per share at 7.5%** to **$305 / $194 per share at 6.5% / 8.5%**. This is an analytical sensitivity, not a prediction of interest rates. [A-001, A-002, OBS-008]

## Interpretation

The strongest near-term macro pressure is input cost rather than financing cost. Apple has a strong balance sheet and relatively limited direct sensitivity to higher interest expense, but its market valuation is duration-sensitive. A premium product mix is a partial operational hedge: IDC says Apple and Samsung gained share as memory costs disrupted lower-end vendors. That resilience may protect unit economics, but absolute component inflation remains a margin headwind. [OBS-008, OBS-009, OBS-010, CON-002]
