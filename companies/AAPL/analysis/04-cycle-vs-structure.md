# Cycle vs Structure — Apple

**Conclusion: Mixed transition.** The handset cycle is weak and components are constrained, while Apple is gaining premium share and continues to compound high-margin Services. The evidence does not support calling this a structural impairment. [OBS-009, OBS-010, CON-002]

## Capital-cycle framing

```text
AI-led memory demand → constrained memory supply / higher BOM
→ OEM pricing and mix choices → device affordability and units
→ hardware gross margin → supplier returns and capacity investment
→ future component supply and pricing
```

The relevant cycle is a component-cost and consumer-device affordability shock, not a conventional Apple capacity build-out. IDC reports a shrinking unit market and sharply higher memory costs, while Apple says the effect may intensify. The structural counterweight is ecosystem-driven Services monetization and premium positioning. [OBS-005, OBS-009, OBS-010]

## Permanent-impairment test

**Cyclical evidence:** industry unit contraction and input-cost spike; Apple retained momentum and gained share in the available data.

**Structural-risk evidence:** EU distribution/payment changes weaken exclusivity in a portion of the ecosystem; no current disclosure quantifies material financial loss.

**Decisive evidence needed:** adjusted gross margin below 46% in FQ4 or below 46.5% in FQ1, Services growth below company growth, or disclosed EU App Store economic leakage. Until then, classify as mixed transition rather than structural decline. [F-002, G-001]
