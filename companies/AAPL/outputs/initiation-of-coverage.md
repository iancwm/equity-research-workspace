# Apple Inc. (AAPL) — Preliminary Initiation of Coverage

**Date:** September 9, 2026 · **Rating:** No benchmark-relative rating — preliminary · **Current price snapshot:** $314.70 · **Base 12-month P/E value:** $320.0 · **Probability-weighted P/E value:** $313.9

## Investment thesis

Apple remains a high-quality cash compounder. Its premium hardware installed base feeds a Services business that generated 26.2% of FY2025 revenue but 42.2% of gross profit, while annual cash flow has supported substantial buybacks. [OBS-005, OBS-006, DER-002]

The central question is valuation, not operational survival. FQ3 2026 was excellent: revenue grew 16%, iPhone revenue 22% and Services 12%. However, reported gross margin included roughly 200 bp of tariff refunds, and management identifies intensifying memory, NAND and DRAM cost pressure. We normalize the refund benefit and forecast FY2027 revenue growth of 6.5% with 32.1% EBIT margin. [OBS-001, OBS-002, OBS-003, OBS-009, A-002]

At 36.1x trailing P/E, the stock already discounts durable premium-quality growth. Corrected probability-weighted P/E scenarios produce $313.9 per share, effectively the current price, while the illustrative DCF gives $237.2. The gap is too wide, and the peer/consensus/DMA-exposure work too incomplete, to issue a benchmark-relative rating. This is not a personalized recommendation. [OBS-012, CON-004, G-001]

## Company overview

Apple sells iPhone, Mac, iPad, Wearables/Home/Accessories and Services globally. iPhone is its largest disclosed product pool; Services is the key mix and margin driver. FY2025 revenue was $416.2B, EBIT $133.1B and simple CFO-less-capex FCF $98.8B. [OBS-004, OBS-005, OBS-006]

## Industry and competitive landscape

The handset market is in a component-cost shock. IDC says global smartphone shipments fell 7.4% YoY in Q2 2026, yet Apple gained share alongside Samsung, consistent with premium-mix and supply-scale advantages. Apple’s own filing says higher advanced-semiconductor, NAND and DRAM costs may intensify. The appropriate view is relative resilience rather than immunity. [OBS-009, OBS-010, CON-002]

Apple’s ecosystem provides switching costs and Services monetization, but EU DMA obligations permit alternative stores, web distribution, alternative payments and steering. Apple retains commission structures under its revised EU terms, but it does not disclose the relevant EU App Store revenue pool; this is a material unquantified risk. [OBS-011, R-002]

## Catalysts, risks and falsification

**Catalyst:** premium-device share gains plus Services growth above total-company growth. [CAT-001]

**Risks:** further memory-cost inflation, demand effects from pricing, an EU take-rate/compliance drag, and a valuation reset if durable growth disappoints. [R-001, R-002]

**Falsification:** reassess the base case if FQ4 adjusted gross margin is below 46%, if FQ1 remains below 46.5%, or if Services growth falls below total revenue growth. [F-002]
