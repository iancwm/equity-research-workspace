# Module 4 — Cycle vs Structure

Apply extra scrutiny because confusing cyclical weakness with structural impairment can reverse the recommendation.

## Analyze

Map the capital cycle:

Demand → Pricing → Margins → Returns → Investment → Capacity → Supply Balance → Pricing

Identify current position.

Select 3–5 leading indicators and record current signal, historical lead, bull interpretation, and bear interpretation.

Estimate supply pipeline where relevant:

existing capacity + announced capacity + under construction - likely closures = future effective capacity

Separate secular narrative significance from measurable financial significance for forces such as AI/automation, regulation, demographics, localization, environmental constraints, or technology substitution.

Run a permanent-impairment test:

- cyclical weakness indicators;
- structural impairment indicators;
- decisive evidence required to distinguish them.

Conclude: Cyclical Weakness / Structural Impairment / Mixed / Insufficient Evidence.
