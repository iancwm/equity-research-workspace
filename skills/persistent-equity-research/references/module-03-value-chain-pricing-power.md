# Module 3 — Value Chain & Pricing Power

Determine who captures economic value and who absorbs pressure.

## Analyze

- competitor count and concentration where available;
- barriers to entry/exit, switching costs, scale, network effects, regulation, IP;
- supplier concentration, substitutability, terms, lead times, scarcity;
- customer concentration, procurement power, switching costs, contract duration, pricing mechanisms;
- pricing-power stress test: ability to price above input inflation while preserving volumes and market share;
- at least one historical stress period where evidence permits;
- where margin compression lands: supplier / company / distributor / customer;
- durable moat vs cyclical advantage.
