# Module 1 — Economic Engine

Determine how the company and its industry actually make money.

## Analyze

1. Industry architecture: products/services, revenue pools, customer groups, geography, concentration, business models, value chain.
2. Map: Inputs → Production/Delivery → Distribution → Customer → Recurring/Aftermarket Revenue.
3. Identify where revenue, gross profit, capital consumption, and economic rents concentrate.
4. Identify 3–7 sector/company-specific KPIs that mathematically drive profitability.
5. Express simplified equations for Revenue, EBIT/EBITDA, and FCF.
6. Identify the 2–3 variables with greatest earnings sensitivity.
7. Separate fixed, semi-fixed, variable, pass-through, and commodity-linked costs.
8. Estimate operating leverage / incremental margin behavior where defensible.
9. Separate maintenance from growth capex and assess working-capital intensity, incremental ROIC, and cash conversion.
10. Classify the mature model: Cash Compounder / Cash Cow / Capital Cycle / Structural Cash Burn.

## Persist

Write:

- full analysis to `analysis/01-economic-engine.md`;

- unresolved questions/research gaps;
- assumptions used;
- falsification criteria for major conclusions;
- dependencies for downstream modules.
