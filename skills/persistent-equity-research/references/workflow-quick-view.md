# Workflow — Quick View

Use when the user wants a fast first-pass view rather than a full IOC.

## Steps

1. Resolve ticker, benchmark, special question, and rating convention.
2. Retrieve only the minimum current information needed for:
   - valuation setup;
   - recent earnings/guidance;
   - central investor debate;
   - one or two structural issues.
3. Return:
   - preliminary stance: constructive / neutral / cautious;
   - what appears priced in;
   - 3–5 critical facts;
   - 2–4 hypotheses worth testing;
   - what evidence would justify upgrading to Full Initiation.
4. Persist source records and facts only if a company workspace already exists or the user elects to continue.

Target: deliver useful signal before doing expensive research.
