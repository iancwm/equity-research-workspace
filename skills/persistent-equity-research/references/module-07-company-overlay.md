# Module 7 — Company Overlay & IOC Forecasts

Translate sector/industry findings into company-specific forecasts and recommendation logic.

## Company architecture

- segment and geographic revenue mix;
- customer/end-market exposure;
- recurring vs transactional / project revenue;
- acquisition contribution vs organic growth;
- major company-specific advantages and vulnerabilities.

## Historical financials

At minimum capture where available:

- revenue;
- organic / reported growth;
- gross / EBITDA / EBIT or operating margin;
- EPS;
- CFO / FCF;
- capex;
- working capital;
- ROIC/ROE where useful;
- net debt / cash;
- share count and capital returns.

## Forecast

Create explicit base-case assumptions for the forecast horizon. Separate company guidance, consensus, and analyst assumptions.

Reconcile:

Revenue → Profit → Tax/interest → EPS → CFO → Capex → FCF

## Recommendation

Evaluate:

- earnings direction;
- valuation expectations;
- balance-sheet/capital-allocation risk;
- probability-weighted bear/base/bull outcomes;
- catalysts within 12–24 months;
- benchmark-relative opportunity cost.

Persist to `analysis/07-company-overlay.md`, `data/`, `state/assumptions.json`, and the ledger.
