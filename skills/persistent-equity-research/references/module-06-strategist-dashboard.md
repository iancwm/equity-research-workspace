# Module 6 — Strategist Dashboard

Convert the validated research into an investor monitoring framework. Do not introduce unsupported narratives.

## Required outputs

### Investment regime
Classify: Early Cycle / Mid Cycle / Late Cycle / Downcycle / Recovery / Structural Growth / Structural Decline / Mixed Transition.

Give three supporting datapoints.

### Earnings direction
6–12 month revision risk: Positive / Neutral / Negative. Identify dominant driver.

### Bull catalyst
Single most important measurable development required for outperformance.

### Bear risk
Single measurable variable most capable of breaking the constructive thesis.

### Key monitor
One datapoint for next quarter:

- current value;
- expected direction;
- bull threshold;
- bear threshold.

### Variant perception
For 2–3 hypotheses:

Consensus → Variant View → Evidence Needed → Investment Consequence
