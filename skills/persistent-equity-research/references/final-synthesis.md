# Final synthesis

Use the validated active research ledger, forecasts, valuation outputs, strategist dashboard, contradictions, and research gaps.

Do not conduct broad new research during synthesis. If a recommendation-critical gap is discovered, stop synthesis, route that gap back to the relevant research node, then resume.

Reconcile:

- macro vs bottom-up evidence;
- demand vs supply;
- cyclical vs structural explanations;
- earnings direction vs valuation expectations;
- company quality vs price paid;
- absolute valuation vs benchmark-relative opportunity cost.

Output internally consistent conclusions for:

1. Three Things That Matter Most — exactly three bullets.
2. What the Market Is Pricing.
3. What Could Be Mispriced.
4. What Would Change the View — explicit falsification criteria.
5. Research Gaps.
6. Rating and price target / valuation range.
7. One-paragraph final thesis: cycle position, earnings direction, valuation setup, main catalyst, main risk, and key monitor.

The synthesis should be reconstructable from persistent state; do not introduce unsupported narrative flourishes.
