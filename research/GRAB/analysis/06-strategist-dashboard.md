# Module 6 — Strategist Dashboard

_Ledger references: DASH-01 to DASH-08, CAT-01, CAT-02, RISK-01 to RISK-04. Sources: S001, S002, S007, S008, S010._

## Investment regime

**Structural Growth with Mid-Cycle profitability inflection.**

Supporting datapoints:
1. 18 consecutive quarters of Adjusted EBITDA growth through Q2 2026.
2. First full-year net profit in FY2025, after 13 years of cash burn since founding (2012) / IPO (2021).
3. FY2026 guidance raised mid-year at the Q2 2026 print (both revenue and Adjusted EBITDA ranges lifted), a positive- rather than merely stable-momentum signal.

## Earnings direction (6-12 months)

**Positive.** Dominant driver: continued Deliveries advertising monetization and Mobility operating leverage, reinforced by SuperBank/Stash consolidation contributing incremental Financial Services revenue that consensus may not yet fully reflect.

## Bull catalyst

Two consecutive quarters of Deliveries Adjusted EBITDA/GMV margin expansion from the current 2.0-2.3% level without a corresponding deceleration in Deliveries GMV growth below ~18% YoY constant currency — confirming pricing power holds through the Foodpanda-ownership-uncertainty window.

## Bear risk

A confirmed adverse outcome from the Vietnam National Competition Commission's review of Grab's commission/fare policies (opened September 2026) — e.g., a mandated commission cap or forced fare-transparency regime — especially if referenced or replicated by regulators in Indonesia, Philippines, or Thailand.

## Key monitor for next quarter (Q3 2026)

**Deliveries Segment Adjusted EBITDA margin (% of GMV).**
- Current value: 2.3% (Q2 2026).
- Expected direction: gradual improvement, per management guidance and trend.
- Bull threshold: ≥2.5% with constant-currency GMV growth sustained ≥18% YoY.
- Bear threshold: <2.0%, or constant-currency GMV growth decelerating below 15% YoY — either would signal renewed competitive intensity.
