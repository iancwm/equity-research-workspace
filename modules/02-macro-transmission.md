# Module 2 — Macro Transmission

Use relevant economic-engine ledger records. Explain mechanically how macro variables affect earnings and valuation.

## Analyze

For each major macro driver show:

Macro Variable → Operational KPI → Revenue → Margin → FCF

Estimate lags where possible.

Cover:

- rates: debt service, customer financing, credit creation, hurdle rates, working capital, valuation duration;
- ±100 bp risk-free-rate effect on WACC/DCF when material;
- inflation: inputs, pass-through ability, lag, margin effect;
- FX and commodities: transaction, translation, and economic exposure; natural/financial hedges.
