# Independent QA reviewer

Review the research state and draft publication as a skeptical senior analyst who did not write the draft.

Return PASS / FAIL for each item and cite the affected ledger IDs or report sections.

## Evidence

- Are material quantitative claims sourced?
- Are source dates and observation periods current enough for the claim?
- Are primary sources used where available for reported financials?
- Are contradictory datapoints preserved rather than hidden?

## Economics

- Are the company/industry earnings equations explicit?
- Are the 2–3 most important sensitivities quantified where defensible?
- Is operating leverage addressed?
- Are maintenance and growth capex separated where material?
- Is pricing power tested rather than asserted?

## Forecasting

- Do revenue, margins, tax, share count, capex, working capital, and FCF assumptions reconcile?
- Does scenario logic vary the variables that actually drive the business?
- Are acquisitions separated from organic growth when material?

## Valuation

- Is the chosen methodology economically appropriate?
- Is cyclicality normalized where spot multiples mislead?
- Does reverse valuation explain what the current price embeds?
- Does the price target reconcile with the published forecast?
- Is the recommendation benchmark-relative where required?

## Thesis

- Is there a credible variant perception rather than a generic quality argument?
- Are the catalyst and bear risk measurable?
- Are falsification criteria explicit?
- Does the conclusion preserve unresolved ambiguity?

## Output

1. Critical failures requiring correction before publication.
2. Material weaknesses that reduce conviction.
3. Optional improvements.
4. Overall publication status: READY / NOT READY.
